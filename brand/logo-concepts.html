MONO & CO. — LOGO CONCEPTS

Each concept on light, on dark, and at small sizes (favicon / social avatar).

A — M& MONOGRAM

A tall condensed M with the blue ampersand tucked against it. Same letterforms as the hero, so the logo and site feel like one piece. Works as a stand-alone mark.

B — EDITORIAL MASTHEAD

The name stacked like a magazine cover title, framed by two rules, with the ampersand oversized in blue. Feels like a publication — confident and premium.

C — ONE & COMPANY

A symbol built on the name: a solid circle (mono — one) overlapping an outlined circle (co — together), with the shared space in blue. Says "strategy and design, one team". Most distinctive and easy to recognise small.

D — STUDIO STAMP

A black square stamp: MONO / CO. stacked inside, a blue ampersand breaking out of the corner. Bold and graphic — great on social, stickers and business cards.
